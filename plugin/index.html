0. Prepare client template for Data Merge (optional)

Open the client's .indd (layers "text", "footer", "Image"), pick the Excel sheet or a data source CSV, then click Prepare. The client file is never edited: a copy <name>_DataMerge.indd is saved next to it, opened and prepared. On every page the "text" frame's 1st paragraph becomes the headline placeholder and its 2nd the DANIS/SERVICE subline, the "footer" frame (optional) becomes DANIS ADD ON and the "Image" frame becomes MainImage — keeping the client's styling. An Excel file is first converted to <name>_DataMerge.csv next to it; image paths may stay empty — they aren't needed to prepare the template.
